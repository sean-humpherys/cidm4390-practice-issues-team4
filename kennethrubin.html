Who is Kenneth S. Rubin? What does he know about Scrum?

Kenneth S. Rubin is an Agile and Scrum educator, coach, and author. He founded Innolution, LLC, a company that helps organizations improve how they develop products using Agile practices. Rubin is best known for writing "Essential Scrum: A Practical Guide to the Most Popular Agile Process," a book that explains Scrum concepts for both beginners and experienced professionals.

Rubin has extensive experience working with Scrum teams and helping organizations adopt Agile methods. He has served as both a Product Owner and Scrum Master, giving him practical knowledge of how Scrum works in real projects. He was also the first managing director of the Scrum Alliance, an organization dedicated to helping people learn and use Scrum.

Through his book, training, and coaching, Rubin teaches important Scrum concepts such as Sprint Planning, Product Backlogs, Daily Scrums, and Sprint Reviews. His work explains how teams can collaborate, adapt to changes, and deliver valuable products through short development cycles called Sprints. His practical experience and educational resources make him a useful source for people who are learning Scrum or looking to improve their team's performance.
