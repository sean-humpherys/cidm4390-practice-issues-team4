What is Sprint execution?

Sprint execution is the phase of the Scrum framework in which the development team works on the items selected in the Sprint Backlog to achieve the Sprint Goal. During this phase, team members collaborate to design, develop, test, and integrate the product features or improvements planned during Sprint Planning. The team follows the Sprint plan while adapting to challenges and new information that may arise. Sprint execution typically takes place over a fixed period, called a Sprint, which is one month or less.

During Sprint execution, the Developers collaborate daily, often using the Daily Scrum to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adjust their plan as needed. The Scrum Master helps the team understand and apply Scrum, supports effective collaboration, and helps address impediments, while the Product Owner clarifies requirements and priorities when necessary. The team also focuses on maintaining quality and ensuring that completed work meets the Definition of Done.
