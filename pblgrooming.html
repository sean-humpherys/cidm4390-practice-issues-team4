What is Product Backlog Grooming?

Product backlog grooming, now usually called backlog refinement, is the ongoing Agile practice of keeping the product backlog clear, ordered, and ready to work from. The team clarifies items, adds acceptance criteria, breaks large stories into sprint-sized pieces, estimates effort, reprioritizes based on value and new information, and removes anything that’s no longer relevant.

The Product Owner leads refinement, with developers contributing questions, technical input, and estimates. It isn’t a formal Scrum event, but many teams hold a session once or twice per sprint and spend roughly 10% of their capacity on it. The payoff is faster sprint planning, fewer mid-sprint surprises, and a team that stays focused on the most valuable work.
