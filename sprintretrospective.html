What is a Sprint Retrospective activity?

The Sprint Retrospective is the final event of each sprint, held after the Sprint Review. Its purpose is to help the team improve how it works, focusing on quality and effectiveness rather than the product itself. The team looks back on the sprint in terms of people, interactions, processes, tools, and its Definition of Done. Together they discuss what went well, what problems came up, and how those problems were or weren’t solved.

The whole Scrum Team takes part, including the Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers, and the session is usually kept to the team so people can speak openly. It’s timeboxed to a maximum of three hours for a one-month sprint and is usually shorter for shorter sprints. The outcome is a short list of concrete, actionable improvements, and the most important ones can be added to the next Sprint Backlog so they actually get done. It isn’t meant for assigning blame; the goal is continuous improvement from one sprint to the next.
